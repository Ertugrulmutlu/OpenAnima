A desktop overlay engine, not just a GIF player.

OpenAnima combines a reusable asset library, overlay inspector, sprite and animation workflows, safe recovery tools, and playful motion controls in one Windows-focused desktop app.

Independent overlays

Place multiple animated assets on the desktop and tune each one with its own scale, opacity, speed, visibility, lock, click-through, and always-on-top controls.

Asset pack workflow

Import single files, folders, folder packs, or zip packs, then switch between organized collections from the Library.

Smart asset setup

Review detected asset types and configure sprites, frame folders, videos, or HUD elements before they enter your library.

Overlay actions

Turn overlays into useful desktop objects by attaching actions that open files, folders, links, or apps when triggered.

Movement and physics

Add velocity, gravity, friction, and screen-edge bounce so companions, sprites, and HUD pieces can feel alive.

Multi-monitor support

Move animated overlays across multiple displays and restore them safely when your screen layout changes.

Recovery tools

Recover hidden, locked, click-through, or off-screen overlays from Settings, Diagnostics, and tray recovery actions.

Local-first desktop app

Your assets, packs, and runtime state stay on your Windows machine, built for personal desktop customization.

Why OpenAnima?

OpenAnima is for people who want more than a looping image on the desktop: it is a small creative layer for animated assets, recoverable customization, and playful workflows.

More than GIFs.

OpenAnima is built around a broad local asset workflow: simple image files, classic GIFs, modern animated formats, game sprites, spritesheets, frame folders, and layered HUD compositions.

WebMVideo overlays for richer animated assets, creator visuals, and ambient desktop pieces.
Static imagesPNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP files for simple transparent or non-transparent overlays.
Frame foldersFolder-based animations for frame-by-frame characters and effects.
Sprite stripsHorizontal or vertical sprite strips with playback, crop, loop, and preview setup.
SpritesheetsGrid-based spritesheet assets for game-style animations.

A cleaner workflow from import to overlay.

The current version is built around a simple repeatable loop: import, verify, place, customize, recover.

What can you build with OpenAnima?

OpenAnima is a small visual layer for playful overlays, desktop toys, creator assets, and interface experiments that live directly above your normal workspace.

Desktop companions

Add animated mascots, pixel characters, and small visual toys that can sit, move, bounce, or idle on your desktop.

Example: ghost, cat, robot, mascot, pixel character

Game-style HUDs

Build overlays that feel pulled from a game interface, then place them beside your work, stream, or play space.

Example: HP bar, mana bar, stamina bar, combo counter

Creator visuals

Add reusable visual markers and animated elements to recordings, tutorials, project updates, and devlog footage.

Example: tutorial markers, recording overlays, devlog visuals

Productivity experiments

Prototype small desktop widgets that communicate state without taking over your workflow.

Example: focus timer, break reminder, ambient indicator
